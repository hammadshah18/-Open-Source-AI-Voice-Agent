ShopVerse Customer Policies

Customer Rights

As a ShopVerse customer, you have the right to:

Receive accurate product descriptions, pricing, and availability information

Transparent communication regarding orders, shipping, and delivery timelines

Request refunds or replacements according to our return policy

Protection of your personal and payment information

Access customer support without discrimination or bias

Receive invoices and order confirmations for every purchase

File complaints or feedback without fear of retaliation

Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

Providing accurate account, shipping, and payment information

Reviewing product descriptions and seller details before purchase

Using promo codes and discounts according to their terms

Respectful communication with customer support and sellers

Ensuring availability to receive deliveries

Following platform rules and applicable laws

Order & Cancellation Policy
Placing Orders

Orders can be placed via the ShopVerse website or mobile app. Once confirmed, an order acknowledgment is sent via email or SMS.

Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped

Once shipped, cancellation is not possible, but a return may be requested

Some items (custom, digital, or final sale) are non-cancellable

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Standard Shipping: 3–7 business days

Express Shipping: 1–3 business days

Same-Day Delivery: Available in select locations

Delivery timelines may vary due to weather, customs, or logistics issues.

Return & Refund Policy
Eligibility

Returns accepted within 7–30 days (product-dependent)

Item must be unused and in original packaging

Certain items are non-returnable (final sale, hygiene items, digital goods)

Refund Method

Original payment method

Store credit (for gift card purchases or promotional items)

Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days after inspection.
